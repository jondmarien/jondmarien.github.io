Why do you want to become an ISSessions Executive?
------------
I have been around Sheridan and ISS since September of 2017. I took a 2-year gap in between 4th and 5th semester. I remember when ISSessions used Matrix as our community to talk to, and when we shifted over to the use of Discord. While I rarely attended meetings in previous years, I wish I had, as I would have been much more ahead than where I am now. I believe that supporting the club as an executive would help further my knowledge in extreme amounts that I am unlikely to receive by just going through this degree. On top of all that, I really enjoy helping students (my fellow classmates, prospecting students, or even new students in our program). I manage my year's discord, along with a fellow classmate, Durval. We create new channels for each semester, and also any other important channels. We make announcements for new technology, articles, or any cool technology thing related to our program. We try to have correct knowledge of most Sheridan things, and I find a lot of classmates dm me to confirm certain things, which is great, because I love to help!

I always try to make sure others understand and are having a good time/enjoying themselves. I also enjoy computers all the way through, down to the transistors on the chips, all the way to new technology that we haven't even developed yet. I love computer security, and have been around computers since as long as I can remember. I love programming, and started with VB when I was ~7. I love finding out how things work, and how to make new things.

What do you hope to change as an exec?
---------------
I hope to bring a positive vibe and good times to the club. I hate unneeded negativity, and always want to push my fellow classmates or co-workers to be the best version of themselves. This is in all aspects of my life! One specific idea I would love to bring to the team is focused on the CTF. I would love to propose a scoreboard freeze each day and/or have different challenges for each day. This way, teams that performed well on Day 1 would not get shifted down the leaderboard for Day 2. Then, we could combine the scores of each day. Or some other kind of resolution could take place. Either way, this is my idea that I would love to provide for the new exec team.

-------------------
This is a really hard question! I would think that I would either be the Transport Layer, or the Session Layer. I could even be the Presentation Layer.

I say transport layer because I like to carefully plan and organize things to make sure I don't lose anything in the process. Session Layer, possibly, because I like to start and end conversations. I know a lot of people have issues with starting conversations, but I love to. Finally, I could be the Presentation Layer as I really do enjoy breaking down complex topics into simple, easy to understand forms. I always say, if you can't explain your topic to a 7 year old, then do you really know what you are talking about? If you can't get a literal child to understand what you mean, then do you even understand? 

I hope it was okay I answered for a few different layers. I really couldn't pick!
