ISSessions Exec Application - 2024/25
The time has come to elect a new group of motivated Sheridan students to lead ISSessions!

We are encouraging anyone to apply. We are actively looking for people that are looking to contribute in any of the following roles:

Producer:
- Handle event live streaming
- Perform necessary video editing
- Setup audio tech for events

Community Manager:
- Student outreach/engagement
- Make and post announcements
- Social media/website Management

Event Manager:
- Event planning for sessions and workshops
- Organize content team
- Present content to members

Content Developer:
- Creating and presenting content for sessions and workshops
- Research news stories and infoSec topics to present

CTF Coordinator:
- Contact sponsors
- Organize logistics of CTF
- Promote the CTF

CTF Challenge Development Lead:
- Manage challenge development
- Make sure challenge development is on track
- Make challenges

CTF Challenge Developer:
- Make challenges for ISSessions CTF

CTF Infrastructure Developer:
- Develop and maintain our CTFd platform
- Create & Deploy docker containers on the cloud
- Deploy cloud infrastructure as code (Terraform)
- DNS configuration
- Work with Kubernetes to ensure efficient and scalable deployment

President:
- Collaborate and lead ISSessions exec team
- Communicate with Sheridan faculty, Sheridan Student Union, current/prospective club members and industry professionals
- Take initiative to set club's agenda
- Ensure all club requirements are met

All roles:
- Attend exec meetings
- Actively looking for speakers to present at ISSessions
- Willingness to work on the CTF prep
